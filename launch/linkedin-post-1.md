# LinkedIn post 1 — the story + how to use it (publish after repo + site are live)

I almost missed my thesis deadline over formatting — not research, formatting.

Purdue's thesis format review is strict: fonts, margins, heading numbering, caption order, figure placement — and accessibility on top of it. Word won't check any of that for you. So I built the tool I wish I'd had: upload your thesis Word file, and it tells you every formatting and accessibility problem, each with what the handbook actually wants instead.

- 85 checks from the handbook and template, plus ADA/WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility (alt text, chart contrast, heading structure, reading order)
- Deterministic: same file, same report, every time. No AI guessing.
- Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/mogiiee/thesis-er
- Try it in your browser, nothing to install: https://mogiiee.github.io/thesis-er/

If it saves you a rejected submission, a star on the repo helps other students find it. And if your school isn't Purdue — the rules are config files, so you can plug in your own university's requirements.

Unofficial community project, not sponsored by Purdue. Always confirm against the official handbook before submitting. Good luck, fellow thesis writers.
